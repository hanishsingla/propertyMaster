# Marketing pack — PropertyMaster (working name)

> Copy is written to be name-swappable. Where a name appears it uses the
> recommended brand **Thikana**; replace globally if another name is chosen.
> Domain notes are DNS-signal only (checked 2026-07-04 against 1.1.1.1) —
> always confirm at a registrar before committing, and do a trademark search
> (ipindia.gov.in, class 36/37) before buying.

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** Thikana is a modern Indian property marketplace where buyers,
renters, and agents find each other — residential and commercial, with honest
listings, map-first search, and prices in plain ₹.

**Audience:**
- Primary: urban home-seekers (buy or rent) in India, 25–45, mobile-heavy.
- Secondary: independent agents and small agencies who need a clean place to
  publish and manage listings (the product already has agent profiles +
  self-serve listing management).
- Tertiary: commercial seekers — shops, offices, warehouses, farm land
  (a differentiator vs. residential-only portals).

**Differentiators to lead with:**
1. One place for *every* property type — flats and villas to shops, offices,
   warehouses, and agricultural land.
2. Map-first search (Leaflet) — browse by locality, not by ad rank.
3. Real agents, real profiles — browse the agent directory, contact directly.
4. Clean, fast, no clutter — a modern SPA, no interstitials, no paywalled
   contact-unlocking (position against 99acres/Magicbricks fatigue).

## 2. Name & domain

> Constraint (user): keep syllable count as low as possible — 1–2 syllables.

### Recommended: **Jagah** — `jagah.com`
"Jagah" (जगह) = *place / space* — two syllables, universally understood, and
the only short word that covers homes AND shops/offices/land equally well.
Tagline pairs: **"Apni jagah, yahin."** / "Find your jagah."
- `jagah.com` — **no DNS record** (good sign; verify at registrar)
- `jagah.in` — taken (check who holds it; could be a conflict or a buy)
